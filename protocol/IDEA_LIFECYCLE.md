# Idea Lifecycle

`GENERATED → NORMALIZED → NOVELTY_HOLD/PASS/REJECT → IMPLEMENTED → SMOKE → PILOT → MEDIUM → CONFIRMATORY → PROMOTE/HOLD/REJECT`

A scientific failure is recorded as a lesson and may cause a mechanism family to be deprioritized. A technical failure may be repaired after identifying the cause. When two distinct candidates from one mechanism family fail scientifically, generate a structurally different family before trying another coefficient tweak.
